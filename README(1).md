## Start Here

A practical path from idea to startup:

1.  **Identify a real problem.** Speak with potential customers and
    document their current alternatives.
2.  **Validate demand.** Test assumptions with interviews, a landing
    page, prototype, pilot, or pre-orders where appropriate.
3.  **Define the business.** Specify the target customer, value
    proposition, pricing hypothesis, and business model.
4.  **Build a focused MVP.** Solve one important problem before
    expanding the feature set.
5.  **Launch and learn.** Measure activation, retention, conversion,
    customer feedback, and revenue.
6.  **Set up the basics.** Address company structure, contracts,
    accounting, privacy, security, and tax obligations.
7.  **Find repeatable growth.** Test a small number of sales and
    marketing channels and track their results.
8.  **Choose funding deliberately.** Compare bootstrapping, revenue,
    grants, incubators, angel investment, and venture capital.

## Founder Checklists

### Before Building

-   [ ] Define the target customer and the problem.
-   [ ] Interview potential customers and document their current
    alternatives.
-   [ ] Research competitors and substitutes.
-   [ ] Write a clear value proposition and pricing hypothesis.
-   [ ] Test demand with a landing page, prototype, or pilot.
-   [ ] Estimate market size using explicit assumptions.

### Before Launch

-   [ ] Test the core user journey with real users.
-   [ ] Set up analytics, error monitoring, and feedback collection.
-   [ ] Review privacy, security, access controls, and backups.
-   [ ] Publish appropriate terms, privacy information, and support
    details.
-   [ ] Test payment, billing, and refund workflows.
-   [ ] Define measurable launch goals.

### Before Fundraising

-   [ ] Prepare a concise pitch deck and product demo.
-   [ ] Document traction, retention, revenue, and other relevant
    metrics.
-   [ ] Build a realistic financial model and use-of-funds plan.
-   [ ] Organize company documents, contracts, IP records, and cap
    table.
-   [ ] Research investor stage, sector, geography, and cheque size.
-   [ ] Review valuation, dilution, rights, and legal terms before
    signing.

### Before Scaling

-   [ ] Track retention, acquisition cost, conversion, revenue, and cash
    runway.
-   [ ] Identify repeatable sales and marketing channels.
-   [ ] Document processes and ownership.
-   [ ] Review tax, regulatory, privacy, and employment obligations.
-   [ ] Set spending controls and business continuity plans.
