How to Start a Startup From Scratch

A simple starting sequence:

    Identify a specific problem experienced by a real group of people.
    Talk to potential customers and check whether the problem matters to them.
    Research existing alternatives and competitors.
    Test the idea with a small experiment before building too much.
    Define the smallest useful version of the product (MVP).
    Build, launch, and collect feedback from early users.
    Improve the product based on evidence and measure whether people return or pay.
    Document what you learn and repeat the process.

This is a learning framework, not a guarantee of startup success. The right approach depends on the market, product, budget, and founder.
